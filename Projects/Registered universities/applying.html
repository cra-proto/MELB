Registered universities outside Canada

Apply for registration – Registered universities outside Canada

If your institution outside Canada regularly enrols Canadian students, you may be able to register as a qualified donee.

As a qualified donee, your institution can:

  • Issue official donation receipts that donors can use to claim a tax credit or deduction in Canada
  • Receive gifts from Canadian registered charities
  • Appear on our public list of registered universities outside Canada

Registration criteria

To be eligible, your institution must meet all of the following requirements:

  • Regularly enrol Canadian students
  • Have an admission requirement that is at least equivalent to completing secondary school
  • Be organized for teaching, study, and research at the post-secondary level
  • Have the authority to grant bachelor's degrees or higher (or equivalent) under the laws and academic standards of its country

Your institution must be able to grant these degrees itself. Being affiliated with a degree-granting university is not enough. Granting only associate degrees, diplomas, certificates or other degrees below the bachelor level is also not enough.

You must be able to show that you meet every requirement.

Send your application

To apply, mail us a letter with all the required documents. In the letter, state that you're applying for registration as a university outside Canada.

Include all required documents

Your application must include all of the following.

Information about your institution

  • Legal name, mailing address, physical address and phone numbers
  • Website address (if available)
  • The date your fiscal year ends
  • A list of all current officials, such as directors and trustees
  • General admission requirements

Supporting documents

  • Complete governing documents, such as incorporating documents, any amendments and current bylaws
  • Documents from the educational authority in your country that confirm your institution:
    • Is an institution of higher learning
    • Can grant bachelor's degrees or higher

Canadian students

For each Canadian student who attended in the last 5 years, include:

  • Full name (first, last, and middle initial, if available)
  • Canadian address
  • Date of birth
  • Canadian social insurance number (if available)
  • Each year or semester the student attended

You may need the students' consent to share their information with us.

We can only register your institution once we have a complete application. All information must be in English or French. For documents in other languages, include a certified English or French translation.

Mail your application

An official or authorized representative of your institution must send your application to:

Charities Directorate
Canada Revenue Agency
Ottawa ON K1A 0L5
Canada

Authorize a representative

We can only deal with your institution's officials, such as directors and trustees, or people you authorize.

To authorize someone to handle your application, send us a written authorization signed by at least one official. Mail it to the address above. If you send it separately from your application, include your institution's legal name and, if you have one, your CRA file number.

After you apply

We will review the application to determine whether your institution meets the requirements for registration.

If the application is approved, we will:

  • Send your institution a letter confirming its qualified donee registration as a university outside Canada
  • Add your institution's name, location, and effective date of registration as a qualified donee to the publicly available List of registered universities outside Canada

If your institution does not meet the requirements, we will send it a letter explaining why we denied its application.

Effective date of registration

If approved, your institution's registration as a qualified donee will be effective from the date we received the complete application.

After you're registered

You must keep meeting the registration criteria and your other obligations. Refer to: Operating a registered university

Certifying amounts for Canadian students

Your Canadian students may need you to certify information so they can claim the tuition tax credit. For more information, refer to: Information for educational institutions outside Canada.
