Applying for qualified donee registration as a university outside Canada

If you are a foreign educational institution that ordinarily includes Canadians in its student body and you meet certain criteria, you can apply to become a qualified donee under the Income Tax Act.

Qualified donees can issue official donation receipts for gifts they receive. These receipts allow donors with taxable income in Canada to claim a credit on their Canadian income tax and benefit return, or a deduction on their Canadian corporation income tax return. Qualified donees are also the only entities that Canadian registered charities are allowed to make gifts to. As a registered university outside Canada, you will have your name, location, and registration status included on our publicly available list of qualified donees.

Registration criteria

To be eligible for qualified donee registration as a university outside Canada, your foreign educational institution must ordinarily include Canadian students in its student body and meet all of the following requirements:

  • Regularly enrol Canadian students
  • Have an admission requirement that is at least equivalent to completing secondary school
  • Be organized for teaching, study, and research at the post-secondary level
  • Be authorized to grant degrees of at least the bachelor level or equivalent under the laws and academic standards of the country it is located in

Your institution will not qualify for registration if it either:

  • Only grants associate degrees, diplomas, certificates, or other degrees below the bachelor level or equivalent
  • Is affiliated with a university but does not have the authority to grant degrees at the bachelor level or higher

If you cannot demonstrate that you meet all of these requirements, including regularly enrolling Canadian students, you will not be eligible for registration.

How to apply

To apply, an official or authorized representative of your foreign educational institution should send a letter and all supporting documents to us at the following address:

Charities Directorate
Canada Revenue Agency
Ottawa ON K1A 0L5
Canada

Submitting a complete application

The letter should state that you are applying for qualified donee registration as a university outside Canada. Your submission must include all of the following information about your institution and supporting documents:

  • Identifying information, including its legal name, mailing address, physical address, and phone number(s)
  • Website address (if available)
  • Fiscal period-end
  • A list of all current officials, such as its directors, trustees, and like officials
  • A copy of complete governing documents, such as incorporating documents and any amendments, and current bylaws
  • General admission requirements
  • A copy of documents issued by the appropriate educational authority in your country of residence that confirms your institution:
    • Is one of higher learning
    • Has the authority to grant degrees of at least the bachelor level
  • A list of the Canadian students who attended your institution during the last five years and identification information for each student, including:
    • Full name (first, last, and middle initial, if available)
    • Canadian address
    • Date of birth
    • Canadian social insurance number (if available)
    • Each year or semester the student attended

Note: you may need to contact the Canadian students to get their approval to release this information.

We cannot register an institution as a qualified donee without a complete submission. All the information above is needed for your request to be considered complete, and must be in English or French. Any documents in other languages must be accompanied by a certified English or French translation.

Who we can communicate with

Under the Income Tax Act, we can only communicate with your institution's officials (director, trustee, or like official) or individuals you have authorized us to deal with.

To authorize a representative who is handling the application for your institution, send a written authorization signed by at least one of your officials to the address above. Include your 7-digit CRA file number in the authorization if you submit it separately from your application.

After you apply

We will review the application to determine whether your institution meets the requirements for registration.

If the application is approved, we will:

  • Send your institution a letter confirming its qualified donee registration as a university outside Canada
  • Add your institution's name, location, and effective date of registration as a qualified donee to the publicly available List of registered universities outside Canada

If your institution does not meet the requirements, we will send it a letter explaining why we denied its application.

Effective date of registration

If approved, your institution's registration as a qualified donee will be effective from the date we received the complete application.

Requirements for maintaining registration

To maintain your registration as a qualified donee, you must continue to meet the eligibility criteria as well as other obligations under the Income Tax Act. These include:

  • Ensuring that any official donation receipts you issue meet the requirements of the Income Tax Act and its regulations
  • Keeping books and records that support any official donation receipts you issue, and providing these to us on request

For more information, go to Operating a registered university outside Canada.

Certifying amounts for Canadian students

Students from Canada may need the university to certify information to support their eligibility for a tax credit for qualifying education. For more information, see Information for educational institutions outside Canada.
