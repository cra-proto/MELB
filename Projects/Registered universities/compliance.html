Compliance for registered universities outside Canada

As a qualified donee under the Income Tax Act, a registered university outside Canada has certain obligations. We use a variety of compliance activities to make sure registered universities outside Canada meet these obligations.

Obligations

As a registered university outside Canada, you must:

  • Ensure that you continue to meet the eligibility criteria for registration
  • Keep books and records that demonstrate you meet the requirements under the Income Tax Act, and provide them to us on request
  • Ensure that the official donation receipts you issue meet the requirements of the Income Tax Act and its regulations
  • Ensure that you do not accept a gift or transfer of property on behalf of a suspended qualified donee

If you fail to meet these obligations, you may be penalized, suspended or revoked. For more information on the requirements for maintaining qualified donee registration, go to Operating a registered university outside Canada.

Penalties and suspensions

If your qualified donee registration is suspended, you:

  • Cannot issue official donation receipts at any time for a gift received during the suspension
  • Must inform potential donors about your suspension
  • Are not eligible to receive gifts from registered charities during that period

For a complete list of the intermediate sanctions that apply to qualified donees, go to Penalties and suspensions.

Revocation

We may revoke your registration as a university outside Canada if you:

  • Ask for voluntary revocation
  • No longer meet the requirements for registration

If you are revoked, you are no longer a qualified donee and cannot issue official donation receipts.

Objections

You can file an objection to our decision regarding a notice of:

  • Suspension of your qualified donee registration
  • Intention to revoke your registration

You have 90 days from the date on the notice to file an objection. The notice of objection must be in writing and provide the reasons for the objection and all the relevant facts.

For more information about this process, go to Objections and appeals for registered charities and other qualified donees.
