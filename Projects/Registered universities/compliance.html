Compliance and sanctions – Registered universities outside Canada

As a registered university outside Canada, you have certain obligations. We use a variety of compliance activities to make sure registered universities outside Canada meet these obligations.

On this page

  • Obligations
  • Suspension
  • Revocation
  • Objections

Obligations

You must meet certain obligations to keep your registration. For the full list, refer to: Operating a registered university

If you do not meet these obligations, we may apply penalties, suspend your registration or revoke it.

Suspension

If your qualified donee registration is suspended, you:

  • Cannot issue official donation receipts at any time for a gift received during the suspension
  • Must inform potential donors about your suspension
  • Are not eligible to receive gifts from registered charities during that period

For all the penalties and suspensions that can apply to qualified donees, refer to: Penalties and suspensions.

Revocation

We may revoke your registration as a university outside Canada if you:

  • Ask for voluntary revocation
  • No longer meet the requirements for registration

If we revoke your registration, you are no longer a qualified donee and cannot issue official donation receipts.

Objections

You can file an objection if you receive one of the following notices from us:

  • Notice of suspension of your registration
  • Notice of intention to revoke your registration

You have 90 days from the date on the notice to file an objection. The notice of objection must be in writing and provide the reasons for the objection and all the relevant facts.

For more information about this process, go to Objections and appeals for registered charities and other qualified donees.
