Registered universities outside Canada

A registered university outside Canada is a qualified donee under the Income Tax Act. If you are a foreign educational institution that ordinarily has Canadians in its student body and you meet certain criteria, you can apply to be registered as a university outside Canada. As a registered university outside Canada, you can issue official donation receipts for gifts you receive, and Canadian registered charities can make gifts to you.
