Operating a registered university outside Canada

As a qualified donee under the Income Tax Act, a registered university outside Canada has certain obligations to demonstrate transparency and accountability to Canadians.

On this page

  • Requirements for maintaining qualified donee registration
  • Keeping books and records
  • Issuing official donation receipts
  • Making a change to your organization

Requirements for maintaining qualified donee registration

To maintain registration as a qualified donee, you must continue to meet the eligibility criteria as well as other obligations under the Income Tax Act. These include:

  • Ensuring that any official donation receipts you issue meet the requirements of the Income Tax Act and its regulations
  • Keeping books and records that support any official donation receipts you issue, and providing these to us on request

If you fail to meet these obligations, you may be penalized, suspended or revoked. For more information, go to Compliance for registered universities outside Canada.

Keeping books and records

To maintain your registration, you must keep adequate books and records containing:

  • Information to allow us to verify revenues for which donors can claim tax credits or deductions
  • Information to allow us to confirm that you continue to meet the requirements for qualified donee registration under the Income Tax Act
  • A duplicate of each official donation receipt you issued

You may keep your books and records in your own country, but you must make them available in Canada at our request. They should also be kept in either English or French. If they were originally created in a different language, a translated version must be available.

Books and records include, but are not limited to:

  • Financial statements supporting any donation receipts that are issued
  • Source documents, such as cancelled cheques and bank deposit slips

For more information about maintaining proper books and records, including the types of records that should be kept, retention periods, and electronic records, go to Keeping records.

Issuing official donation receipts

As a registered university outside Canada, you are a qualified donee and can issue official donation receipts for income tax purposes.

Before issuing an official donation receipt, you must determine whether you have received a gift for the purposes of the Income Tax Act and the eligible amount of the gift. For more information, go to What is a gift? and see P113, Gifts and Income Tax.

Official donation receipts must include all information required under the Income Tax Act and its regulations. Learn more about the information that must be on an official donation receipt.

Note: registered universities outside Canada are not required to have or include a registration number (for example, BN 123-456-789 RR0001) for the purpose of issuing official donation receipts.

Making a change to your organization

Your institution should inform us of any changes to its legal name or address so that its information on the list of qualified donees is correct. A donor's receipt may not be allowed if this information does not match the listing.

To provide this information, you can write a letter to us at the following address:

Charities Directorate
Canada Revenue Agency
Ottawa ON K1A 0L5
Canada

The letter needs to include the name and signature of a current official or authorized representative. For us to process the change, you must also send in the legal document effecting the name change, such as an amended incorporation document.
