Registered universities outside Canada

Operating a registered university – Registered universities outside Canada

As a registered university outside Canada, you must meet certain obligations to keep your registration.

On this page

  • Requirements for maintaining qualified donee registration
  • Keeping books and records
  • Issuing official donation receipts
  • Changing your name or address

Requirements for maintaining qualified donee registration

To keep your registration, you must:

  • Continue to meet the registration criteria
  • Keep books and records that support your official donation receipts, and give them to us when we ask
  • Issue official donation receipts that meet the requirements of the Income Tax Act and its regulations
  • Not accept gifts or property for a qualified donee whose registration is suspended

If you do not meet these obligations, we may apply penalties, suspend your registration or revoke it. For more information, go to Compliance and sanctions.

Keeping books and records

To maintain your registration, you must keep adequate books and records containing:

  • Information to allow us to verify revenues for which donors can claim tax credits or deductions
  • Information that shows you still meet the registration requirements
  • A duplicate of each official donation receipt you issued

You may keep your books and records in your own country. When we ask, you must make them available in Canada. Keep them in English or French. If a record is in another language, you must have a translation available.

Books and records include:

  • Financial statements that support your donation receipts
  • Source documents, such as cancelled cheques and bank deposit slips

For details on which records to keep, how long to keep them and electronic records, refer to: Keeping records.

Issuing official donation receipts

As a qualified donee, you can issue official donation receipts.

Before you issue a receipt, you must confirm that what you received is a gift under the Income Tax Act. You must also work out the eligible amount of the gift.

For details, refer to:

  • What is a gift
  • Guide P113, Gifts and Income Tax

Official donation receipts must include all information required under the Income Tax Act and its regulations. Learn more about the information that must be on an official donation receipt.

Unlike registered charities, you don't need a registration number on your official donation receipts.

Changing your name or address

Tell us if your institution's legal name or address changes, so we can keep the list of qualified donees correct. A donor's receipt may not be allowed if this information does not match the listing.

Send a letter to:

Charities Directorate
Canada Revenue Agency
Ottawa ON K1A 0L5
Canada

The letter needs to include the name and signature of a current official or authorized representative. For a name change, also send the legal document that makes the change, such as amended incorporation documents. For an address change, the signed letter is enough.
