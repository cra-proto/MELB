GCWeb design system

A component and pattern library for CRA web content. Use this guide to decide which component to reach for based on what you are trying to help a user do.

Every GCWeb component belongs to one of four families, grouped by the job it does for the user. Pick the family that matches your intent, then pick the component inside it.

Families

Page structure
Give a page a spine — how content is laid out, ordered, and navigated within a single page or across a multi-page flow
Content elements
The building blocks readers see inside sections — typography, links, lists, tables, and text emphasis
Wayfinding
Help users find the right page from a hub or contact the right group when they cannot self-serve
Interaction & feedback
Ask the user a question, warn them of something, or confirm the result of an action

Not sure which family?

Answer these three questions in order. Stop at the first "yes."

1. Are you telling the user something time-sensitive, or reacting to something they just did?

Go to Interaction & feedback.

2. Is the user trying to get from a hub to the right page, or trying to reach the CRA?

Go to Wayfinding.

3. Are you deciding how the whole page is laid out or how a multi-step task is broken up?

Go to Page structure.

Otherwise

You probably need a Content element — the block that renders text, a link, a list, or a table inside a section.
