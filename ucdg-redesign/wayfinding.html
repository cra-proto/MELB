Wayfinding

Components that help a reader get to the right place. Use these when a page's job is to route people onward to the task they need, or to give them a way to reach the CRA when they can't finish online. Wayfinding is about the choice between destinations, not the content at any one of them.

Components in this family

Doormats
A link title plus a short description that helps a reader pick the right destination from a hub, topic, or subway index page
Contact us
How to present contact options — online, phone, and TTY — and how to route people to the right channel when they can't self-serve

Choosing between them

Sending the reader to another page?

Use Doormats — a titled, described link that helps them choose the right destination from a set.

Reader can't finish online?

Use Contact us — the right channels, in the right order, with what they need before they call.
